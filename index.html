Where the value comes from

Expected count per pack × average price of the cards in that category. Within a category every card is treated as equally likely. Riot hasn't said otherwise, but hasn't promised it either.

Opening a display (simulated)

Monte Carlo of 20,000 displays of 24 packs, using the same slot model. EV is an average that a few huge hits pull upward. The median is what a typical box is actually worth.

How it's calculated

  1. Pack layout. 14 cards: 7 Common, 3 Uncommon, 2 Rare-or-better, 1 Foil, 1 Rune/Token. 24 packs per display.
  2. Hits replace Rares. Epics, Alt Arts, Special Arts, Overnumbered, Signature and Ultimate cards take a Rare-or-better slot, so expected Rares = 2 − (all those rates). They are never extra cards on top of the 14.
  3. Ultimates get their own rate. Riot publishes each Ultimate's odds directly: Unleashed's Baron Nashor in "< 0.1% of packs", Radiance's Packed Amphitheater in about 0.025% (≈1 in 4,000). Common mistakes:
    • Putting the Ultimate in the Overnumbered/Showcase pool and splitting one rate evenly. For Radiance this gives about 1 in 1,400 instead of 1 in 4,000, which nearly triples its value.
    • Reading "< 0.1%" as exactly 0.1%. It's an upper bound, so the Baron's contribution here is a ceiling.
    • Applying per-display or per-case odds as if they were per-pack, or assuming one is guaranteed per case. Riot says none is guaranteed in any pack, box or case.
    • Matching by card name. "Baron Nashor (Alternate Art)" at ~$40 is a different product from the Ultimate. This tool matches TCGplayer's (Ultimate) printing.
  4. Prices. Daily TCGplayer snapshot from tcgcsv.com. Rares, Epics and Showcase cards are foil printings, so their foil price is used. Commons/Uncommons use non-foil, except in the foil slot. Promo-only printings are excluded.
  5. Bulk. By default, any card listed under $1 counts as $0, because bulk rarely sells individually at market price. You can change this under Assumptions. Set the cutoff to 0 to count every card at full price.
  6. EV isn't profit. Market price is not what you get selling singles. Use the "you keep" setting for fees, shipping and sell-through. Chase cards also move fast.
